Privacy & Texting Policy

Newesque AI Solutions · Last updated November 10, 2026

What we collect

When you book a call, send a message, or call us, we collect what you give us: your name, business name, phone number, email address (if provided), and what you'd like help with. Calls to our phone line may be recorded and transcribed so we can follow up accurately.

How we use it

Only to respond to you: to confirm and remind you about calls you book, answer your questions, and follow up about the services you asked about. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for their marketing.

Text messages (SMS)

If you check "Text me a confirmation and a reminder" when booking, or agree on a call to receive texts, you agree to receive text messages from Newesque AI Solutions about your appointment and our conversation. These may include confirmations, reminders, and replies to your questions.

Message frequency varies, usually 1–4 messages per booking. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

Mobile numbers and text opt-in information are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run our phone, text, and scheduling systems (for example, our telecom carrier). They only process your information to deliver those services to us.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information at any time by calling or texting (213) 451-5035. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages.
