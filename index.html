    FAQ
    
    
    
      
    Will my customers know it's AI?
    That's up to you. It can introduce itself as your AI assistant, or simply answer as your front desk. Either way it sounds calm and natural. Call (213) 451-5035 and judge for yourself.
    
      
    I'm not good with technology. Will I have to learn anything?
    No. I set everything up and look after it. You get a summary after each call. If you can read a text message, you're all set.
    
      
    Do I need a new phone number?
    No. You keep your number. Calls you don't answer roll over to the receptionist instead of voicemail. We turn that on together in about two minutes.
    
      
    What does it cost? Is there a contract?
    The demo is free. I'll set one up that answers as your business so you can hear it first. If you want to keep it, you'll get a clear price before you commit to anything. It's month to month: no long-term contract, cancel anytime.
    
      
    What if it doesn't work for my business?
    Every setup comes with a 30-day guarantee. If it isn't answering and booking the way you approved in the first 30 days after it goes live, I fix it within a week. If it still isn't right, I cancel it and refund your full setup fee. The details are in the one-page service agreement.
