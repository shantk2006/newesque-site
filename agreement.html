2. Payment

  • Half the setup fee is due when you sign up. It reserves your start date and pays for the setup work. Once setup work has started, it is not refundable, except under the 30-day guarantee below.
  • The other half of the setup fee and your first month are due when it goes live.
  • After that, the monthly fee is charged automatically to the card on file each month (or invoiced by email if you prefer), on the same date it went live.
  • Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

3. 30-day guarantee

If, during the first 30 days after it goes live, the service isn't answering and booking the way you approved, tell us. We'll fix it within 7 days. If it still isn't working the way you approved after that, we'll cancel the service and refund the full setup fee you paid.

The guarantee covers how our service performs. It doesn't cover problems caused by your phone carrier, call forwarding being turned off, or changes to your business information we weren't told about.

4. No long-term contract

Service is month to month. You can cancel anytime by calling, texting or emailing us before your next billing date. You won't be charged again after you cancel. We don't refund partial months.

5. Your part

  • Give us accurate information about your business (hours, services, service area, how you book) and tell us when it changes.
  • Keep call forwarding turned on with your phone carrier. Some carriers charge for it.
  • For AI Lead Management: only send us leads who agreed to be contacted, and keep the consent checkbox we add to your forms.
  • You decide whether the AI introduces itself as an AI assistant.

6. Your information

We use your business information and your callers' information only to run your service. We don't sell it or share it with anyone for marketing. Calls are handled by our telephone and AI providers under their security terms. See our Privacy Policy.

7. Limits

The AI answers from the information you approved and can make occasional mistakes. You're responsible for your own pricing, scheduling and business decisions. Our total responsibility for any claim is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim. We aren't responsible for lost business caused by phone carrier, internet or third-party outages.

8. General

We may update prices or these terms with 30 days' written notice; you can cancel before a change takes effect. This agreement is governed by California law.
